The problem

Customers shouldn't need a different app for every shop.

Bookings live in one place. Menus in another. Receipts, repair updates and loyalty in others. OneQR aims to make the next step obvious—right where the customer is.

01 / TOO MANY TOOLS

Disconnected touchpoints

Customers jump between social pages, phone calls, forms and separate services just to complete a simple task.

02 / LOST FOLLOW-UPS

Good customers don't return

Small businesses can struggle to turn a first visit into a repeat booking, reorder or long-term relationship.

03 / TOO MUCH FRICTION

Digital tools feel complicated

Owners need practical ways to serve customers without replacing every system they already use.

Designed to be useful

A front door, not another walled garden.

The product should work with the way businesses already operate, adding a simple customer interface around their existing tools wherever practical.

One persistent QR

Keep the same printed code while updating the actions, offers and information behind it.

Industry-aware actions

Show the most relevant tasks for a salon, shop, restaurant, garage or repair service.

Useful owner insights

Help owners see which actions customers use and where people stop—not vanity metrics alone.

Works with existing tools

Link to approved payment providers, WhatsApp, booking platforms or business systems where integrations are available.

Secure customer records

Keep private records behind appropriate verification instead of exposing them through a public QR link.

Simple repeat journeys

Make rebooking, reordering and finding a previous service easier—with customer consent and clear controls.
